Banker.AI:

Supports bankers before, during and after every customer conversation, helping them prepare, advise and follow up faster.

**Why it matters:** bankers spend a lot of their time on manual admin like preparing, documenting and following up, when they could be with customers. Banker.AI takes on that work and surfaces the insights that matter, so they can focus on the relationship.

### **How it creates value**

* **Growth** Better sales leads and more relevant product offers.  
* **Customer experience** Faster decisions and clearer answers build trust.  
* **Productivity** Handles notes, call summaries and loan paperwork automatically.

### **Example capabilities**

* Before: a briefing on the customer, drawn from NAB's data, with risks and opportunities to raise.  
* During: live analysis, what-if scenarios, suggested next steps and in-the-moment coaching.  
* After: drafts credit write-ups, call summaries, follow-ups and documentation.

> **Its role** It's where NAB's enterprise AI first reaches customers. The same capability rolls out in stages: first supporting bankers (with a person always in control), then shared between banker and customer, and in time serving customers directly through Customer.AI.

Customer.AI:

A customer-facing AI agent that completes tasks end to end — personalised, proactive and always available.

**Why it matters:** soon, AI agents will let customers compare, switch and act in seconds, wherever they are. Customer.AI is NAB's own agent. Because it knows each customer's situation, history and preferences, customers keep coming to NAB directly rather than going through an outside agent.

### **How it creates value**

* **Customer experience** Handles a request from start to finish across a customer's whole financial life, instead of passing them between disconnected steps.  
* **Growth** Better product matches, more sign-ups and more everyday use.  
* **Strategic position** Keeps NAB dealing with customers directly, instead of an outside AI sitting between them and the bank.

### **Example capabilities**

* Sorting out disputes and service requests.  
* Helping customers choose and sign up for products.  
* Proactive money insights and suggestions.

> **Its role** Over time, the main way customers deal with NAB. It's not a separate product but the same technology behind Banker.AI, extended from supporting bankers to serving customers directly.
